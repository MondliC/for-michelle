FROM A DREAM TO A LETTER

To Michelle ♡From Mondli

Dear Michelle,

I've been trying to figure out how to tell you this without sounding like I'm writing the script for a whole romantic movie. 😂 But I had a dream about you, and I woke up wishing I could spend just five more minutes in it.

We've been talking for some time now. Somewhere between the random conversations, the jokes, and getting to know little pieces of who you are, you've quietly become someone I look forward to hearing from. And then my mind decided to take things a few chapters ahead.

In my dream, we had just bought a beautiful, big house. The kind we'd probably drive past today, point at, and laugh about owning one day. Except this time, we were standing outside it with the keys in our hands. It was ours.

I could see us walking through the front door, still not quite believing it. There were spacious rooms, sunlight coming through huge windows, a kitchen where we'd probably spend more time talking than cooking, and a garden where we could sit together on slow Sunday afternoons.

And the driveway? There were two beautiful cars parked outside. We were teasing each other about whose car was better. I already know you'd have a convincing argument, but I'd still insist mine was the nicer one. 😂

We weren't just celebrating the things we'd bought. We were celebrating everything it had taken to get there — the late nights, the work, the sacrifices, the little victories nobody else had seen. We'd built a life that felt like ours.

I imagined slow Saturday mornings. Music playing while we made breakfast. Coffee going cold because we'd started talking about something completely random. Grocery trips that turned into little dates. Road trips with terrible singing, new places to explore, photos we'd laugh about years later, and evenings spent watching movies until one of us fell asleep halfway through.

I imagined cheering you on when something you'd worked hard for finally happened. And on the days when life was heavy, I imagined us having somewhere safe to bring those feelings home. A place filled not just with furniture and beautiful things, but with laughter, patience, kindness, and peace.

And the strangest thing is, I don't remember every detail of that dream. I just remember how happy I felt standing next to you. Like we had made it — not just financially, but in all the ways that matter.

Now, I know we're still learning about each other. I don't want to rush our story, make promises for the future, or pretend a dream is a plan. But waking up made me realise something I wanted you to know:

It's not just that I like talking to you, Michelle. I like the possibility of a life where we keep choosing to know each other, one day at a time.

Maybe years from now we'll laugh about this letter and the big house with two cars. Or maybe we'll be standing in a driveway somewhere, holding new keys, remembering that I once had a dream that felt a little like that moment.

For now, I'm just grateful our paths crossed. I'm glad I get to know you in real life, not only in my dreams.

And if I could go back to that dream for five minutes, it wouldn't be for the house or the cars. It would be for the feeling of sharing that moment with you.

With a smile and a little too much imagination,
Mondli ♡

P.S. If that dream ever comes true, I'm claiming my parking spot first. We can negotiate the rest. 😂❤️

ONE LAST THING

The best part of the dream was you.

And the best part of waking up? Knowing I can still talk to you.

Whatever tomorrow looks like, I'm glad you're part of my today. ♡
